OPEL

This research was focused on Hall Effect Thruster design. In the later parts of the research, we started looking at LLC converters for a Power Processing Unit and Electron Cyclotron Resonance thrusters ECR. The ECR thruster we were able to achieve 25W at 2.45GHz into the antenna; however we believe we needed more power >40W to get ignition.

COLUMBIA

This work was focused on IC design going from mmWave recievers and LNAs to mixed signal chips for data conversion and finally pure analog design for amplifiers.

OLIN

This work contains most of my projects as an undergraduate ranging from physics and mathematics to robotics and mechanical to IC design for mixed-signal chips in the open source Sky130A using magic, ngspice, and Xschem.
